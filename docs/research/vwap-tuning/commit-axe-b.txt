research: the trend filter the spec asked for is the only coherent candidate, and it stays off

Three filters implemented, all off by default, all measured on the full 59 999-bar dataset with
each candidate split into halves. The deliverable is as much the negative results as the
positive one.

The non-regression control passes: with no filter the shipped rule returns 883 trades, 40,2 %,
-392,74 EUR, PF 0,9314, which is the reference exactly, and the script checks it on every run.

The session filter is real but weak. Overlap alone gives a factor of 1,100 and is positive in
both halves, 1,153 and 1,053, but its t-statistic is 0,58, so the per-trade expectancy cannot be
told apart from zero. The wider window, overlap plus London plus New York, is worse at 0,981 and
inconsistent across halves: adding the hours around the crossing destroys the effect. Left off.

The volume filter is incoherent, and that is its useful finding. At 1,0 times its own average it
degrades to 0,901; at 1,2 times it improves to 1,109. A filter whose sign flips when its
threshold moves by 20 % measures the threshold, not the market. The census says why the
neighbourhood is so sensitive: the median relative volume at the signal is 0,965, so the 1,0
threshold cuts the population through the middle, where the noise is. Left off.

The trend filter is the only coherent result. It would raise the factor from 0,931 to 1,555 and
the net from -392,74 EUR to +331,61 EUR over 117 trades at 53,0 % of wins, with both halves
above 1,5 -- 60 trades at 1,581 and 57 at 1,539. No other candidate does that. Its t-statistic is
2,18, the highest in the table, and the gradient is monotone in the threshold, which is the
signature of information rather than of an accident.

Why it works despite looking contradictory: the single-bar slope is almost always negative
during a pullback, only 6,1 % of the 1 973 signals have a positive one, so it is informative
precisely when it is not. A pullback whose slow average still pushes up is a pause inside an
intact trend, not a reversal.

It stays off, because 117 trades on a single market is not a basis for enabling anything, the
t-statistic is optimistic since trades cluster by regime and only one position runs at a time,
and it comes from sixteen uncorrected comparisons.

This also corrects what this axis told the lead earlier. It had reported that the trend filter
refused 100 % of signals and was structurally incompatible with the rule. That was true on
synthetic data and false on the real market, where it lets 12 % through and those are the right
ones. The module docstring and the passing test were rewritten accordingly, and the retraction
is in the report rather than quietly dropped.

What remains open is stated in the report: the candidate has never seen new data, so it needs
gold, native H1 or an H4 bucketing with the same setting applied without retuning; the
comparisons need a multiple-testing correction; the volume filter should be shaped as a slope
rather than a level; and 1 090 of the 1 973 signals are dropped for lack of a slot, so any
filter measurement is partly a measurement of slot priority.
