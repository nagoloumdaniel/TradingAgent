feat(backtest): the entry_zone gate the backtest never applied, and the spread it never modelled

Two agents worked this in parallel and their results meet in one place: the backtest has been
measuring a market that is not the one the agent trades on.

The first gap is structural. Production refuses a signal whose executable price leaves the
published band -- RM-012, `risk.checks.check_entry_zone`. The harness never applied that gate
and filled at half a spread, so the real effect of `entry_zone_atr` had never been measured by
any campaign. The gate now exists in `BacktestConfig` as `entry_zone_parity`, off by default,
with its basis selectable between the price actually paid and the fill level, and
`BacktestResult.refused_entry_zone` counting the refusals. Off by default because every past
campaign was measured without it and turning it on silently would change what their numbers
mean.

Measured on 8 000 bars with the observed spread: the gate refuses 56 of 128 candidate entries,
44 %, and it refuses mostly losers -- the win rate rises 3,7 points and the profit factor from
0,5816 to 0,7154. It discriminates usefully. But it also becomes impassable when the spread
exceeds half the band, which is what happened on two of the four incident signals, where the
margin was -0.12 USD: even a fill at the exact signal price was refused.

The second gap is worse, and it was not the question being asked. The deposit's cost model
applies 0,5 basis point of spread. The spread actually quoted on the demo account is 18.424 USD,
which is 2,2 basis points -- 4,5 times the model. Switching to it with the gate still off moves
the full-dataset backtest from -392.74 EUR and 0.9314 to -763.60 EUR and 0.8693. Every campaign
this project has run was measured on a market more favourable than the real one, on the single
line item that decides profitability.

The recommendation in the report is therefore to widen the band before touching anything else,
because the gate is useful but a 0,1 ATR band is narrower than the spread on bitcoin -- and to
fix the cost model before either, since tuning a gate while the cost assumption is wrong by a
factor of 4,5 is adjusting the lock on an open door.

Five tests were written before the code and were red first. The width sweep and the remaining
bases were still running when the report was written, and the report says so rather than
implying completeness.
