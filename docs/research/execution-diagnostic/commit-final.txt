fix(backtest): the entry_zone gate judged at the real price is worse than no gate at all

Full dataset, observed spread, real runs. The same gate gives opposite answers depending on
which price it judges, and that is the result.

Gate off: 886 trades, -763.60 EUR, PF 0.8693.
Gate judged at the harness price, half a spread: 807 trades, -422.48 EUR, PF 0.9191. It refuses
169 entries and improves the result, and the entries it refuses are worth -391.17 EUR at a
factor of 0.41. Judged there, the gate is useful.
Gate judged at the real price, the whole ask, which is what production does: 716 trades,
-720.40 EUR, PF 0.8487. Worse than having no gate at all. The 107 entries the full spread flips
are worth +364 EUR, so those are winners being refused.

So the rule as coded does not merely mis-tune in production, it removes value. And the literal
rule that fired on 2026-10-09, where an entry landing exactly on its own signal price was
refused, turns out to sort nothing: 147 refusals, all purchases, factor 0.8676 against 0.8693
for the book.

What the gate usefully catches is the drift of the fill, not the spread. Entries that drifted
more than 13 USD (0.054 ATR) lose at a factor of 0.41; those between 5.7 and 13 USD win. The
current rule cuts at 5.7 USD because the spread eats 76 % of the drift budget -- a band of
0.1 ATR is 48 USD at the median and the spread is 18.424 USD. It cuts well before the threshold
that actually separates good entries from bad.

Three options were measured rather than argued. Widening alone is flat and never beats no gate:
0.15 gives 0.8711, 0.20 gives 0.8780, 0.30 gives 0.8732. Comparing the ask against a band
shifted by the spread is not a fix either: it refuses 0 of 886 entries and is identical to no
gate, which means it disables the gate rather than correcting it. Recalibrating
entry_zone_atr from 0.1 to 0.13 gives 802 trades, -661.98 EUR, PF 0.8748, 185 refusals instead
of 368, and +101.62 EUR against no gate. It restores the drift budget to 13 USD exactly, which
is the measured threshold, and it is a parameter rather than code.

Recommended in that order: fix the cost model first, because 3.5 basis points of missing spread
dwarf every option here; do not disable the gate; set entry_zone_atr to 0.13, and not to 0.2,
because the number comes from a measurement rather than a round step.

The strategy remains unprofitable at 0.8748 against a 1.20 threshold. Parity is necessary and
not sufficient.

One defect is reported rather than hidden: the reference basis had a sign error for sells, and
its first run produced an invalid 711 trades at 0.9252. It was corrected, tested and
re-measured. The gate is off by default, so it never reached production.
