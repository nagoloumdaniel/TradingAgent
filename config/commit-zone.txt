fix(config): widen the bitcoin entry band to the measured drift threshold, leave gold alone

Four of the last six bitcoin signals were refused by `entry_zone`, and the reason was always
the same: the ask landed outside the band. The arithmetic is plain. On BTCUSD a band of 0,1 ATR
is about 19,2 USD while the spread is 18,4, measured from the risk decisions themselves at
18.424 and 19.443. The spread was consuming 96 % of the band budget before the price had moved
a point.

What the gate usefully discriminates is not the spread but the drift between the signal and the
fill. Entries that drifted more than about 13 USD, 0,054 ATR, lose at a profit factor of 0,41;
those between 5,7 and 13 USD win. The rule was cutting at 5,7 USD, well before the threshold
that separates anything. A band of 0,13 ATR puts the drift budget back at exactly 13 USD.

Measured over 59 999 M15 bars under the observed spread: 802 trades, profit factor 0,8748 and
+101,62 EUR, against 0,8487 for the current rule, with refusals falling from 368 to 185.

Gold keeps 0,1, and that is a measurement rather than an oversight. Its spread is 0,31 USD
against a band of 0,78, so 40 % of the budget rather than 96 %, and all five of its risk
decisions were authorised. Applying bitcoin's number to gold would transpose a measurement made
on another market. The two manifests sit side by side in the same directory, so the asymmetry
is written into the file it affects, with its figures.

What this does not fix is the cost model. The deposit charges 0,5 basis points of spread where
the account quoted 2,2, and that gap is worth more than every calibration above. The strategy
also remains unprofitable at 0,8748 against a 1,20 threshold: parity is necessary and not
sufficient.
